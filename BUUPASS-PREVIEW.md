# BUUPASS website preview

This independent website combines the Jambojet-based flight prototype with BUSCAR's copied bus search, route, results, seat-selection, and WhatsApp booking-request flow. The bus and flight routes, schedules, seats, and fares are sample information and are not live inventory.

All pages remain `noindex` while Buupass-specific services are being configured. Bus booking requests use the single WhatsApp number configured for this site, 0745342579. The flight flow remains a design prototype and does not confirm a booking or process payment.

The project is independent and does not include either source project's `.git` directory.
